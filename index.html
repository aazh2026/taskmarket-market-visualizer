Method. Every number on this page is fetched server-side from the public Taskmarket API and cached in data/market.json; the page never calls the API directly from the browser. Endpoints used: /api/market/stats, /api/stats/breakdowns, /api/stats/platform-time-series, /api/stats/activity-heatmap, /api/stats/activity-feed, /api/tasks. Distinct posters per week are computed by the fetcher from the full paginated task list; time to decision is the median gap between a task's creation and expiry over completed tasks; reward concentration is the share of total posted reward held by the ten largest requesters.
